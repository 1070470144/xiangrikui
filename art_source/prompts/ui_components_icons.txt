All assets must be fully visible on a transparent background, with consistent scale language, thin worn gold botanical linework, and restrained glow. No words, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no watermark.
Style: premium hand-painted storybook UI, old gardener field journal plus softly glowing botanical instrument, subtle paper and oxidized brass texture, readable at small size.
Palette: #111827, #25263A, #3A3032, #E5B94B, #FFF0B0, restrained #8E3D4A and #4C8490.
Avoid: modern sci-fi HUD, neon cyberpunk, glassmorphism, ornate medieval filigree overload, saturated candy colors, perspective tilt, fake text, grid lines.
